## The stacking rules that decide whether you actually get paid

Two rules trip people up, and neither shows up in most coupon roundups.

Reward Cash, the $5 certificates you earn from points, is a different animal: it can be applied alongside a promo code in most cases. **Rule two: external coupon codes can kill your cashback.** This is the expensive mistake. Portal terms are blunt about it: if you apply a coupon or deal that isn't posted on the portal's own page, the portal can't guarantee you'll receive cash back on the purchase. A 25% code grabbed from a random aggregator feels free until you realize it silently cancelled the cashback you clicked through for.

The workable habit: open the portal's Famous Footwear page, take the code *from there*, and never open a second tab with a different code. If the portal lists no code, shop the sale price bare — the markdown is usually worth more than 1% anyway.

Also worth knowing: commercial reseller purchases are excluded from cashback across portals, so if you're buying inventory to flip, don't expect the rebate.
